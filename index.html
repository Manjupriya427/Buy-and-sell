How ReMarket works


• Sellers submit used products and expected prices.


• ReMarket reviews products and makes a purchase offer.


• After accepting the offer and handing over the product, ReMarket owns it.


• Buyers purchase products from ReMarket at the displayed resale price.


• ReMarket is responsible for managing the product inventory.


This is an educational project. Data is stored in your browser. It does not process real payments or arrange real delivery.
